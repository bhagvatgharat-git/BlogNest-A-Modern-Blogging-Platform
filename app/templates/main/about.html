About The Platform

A modern home for words that matter.

BlogNest is an internship-grade full-stack publishing platform engineered for simplicity, speed, and clean aesthetic reading.

🎯 Project Philosophy

Modern digital readers are overwhelmed with visual clutter, ads, and convoluted user flows. BlogNest strips away unnecessary noise in favor of a soothing Light Blue & White aesthetic that prioritizes readability, typography, and author empowerment.

Every feature—from automatic reading time estimation to private draft controls and threaded discussions—has been carefully implemented following clean software engineering patterns.

🔒

Robust Security

Werkzeug cryptographic password hashing, session cookies, and route protection with Flask-Login.

⏱️

Smart Reading Time

Real-time word count calculation and automated reading estimation based on human reading speeds.

🛡️

Strict Privacy

Drafts remain private to authors. Only the original author can edit or delete their respective stories.

💬

Community Dialogue

Interactive commenting system with author badges and comment moderation controls.

Engineering Stack

Python Flask • Flask-SQLAlchemy • SQLite • Flask-Login • Jinja2 • Vanilla CSS3 Design System
